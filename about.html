About This Website

This website is created for my Media and Information Literacy (MIL) class project. The task is to create our own website and post information every day.

I chose Anime as the theme because I love watching and learning about different series. Some of my favorite anime are Hunter x Hunter, World Trigger, My Dress-Up Darling, Death Note, Attack on Titan, The Quintessential Quintuplets, More Than a Married Couple, and Monster.

Every day I will post new content related to anime — reviews, thoughts, recommendations, character analysis, or fun facts.
